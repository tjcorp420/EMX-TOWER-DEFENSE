EMX TOWER DEFENSE V6 PHASE 3 - LIGHT UPGRADE

This ZIP intentionally includes code/meta files only. No existing sounds or asset images are included. Keep your current assets/ and sfx/ folders in place.

What changed:
- Campaign maps are now mobile-first 15-wave sprints.
- Elite maps are now 20-wave runs.
- Boss checks happen every 5 waves. The last wave spawns the Final Glitch King.
- Difficulty is compressed so the game ramps faster without dragging into 30+ wave lag.
- Coin rewards, pop rewards, miner income, and end-round bonuses were increased so top-tier branches are reachable within shorter sessions.
- Spend Shards / Meta Upgrade lab expanded with permanent damage, economy, cooldown, crit, cache, lives, starter coins, and shard reward buffs.
- Win/lose screen was rebuilt with EMX glassmorphism, animated tally counters, star pop animations, reward strip, and pulsing CTA buttons.
- Core Shard rewards now scale with star rating and Shard Amplifier upgrades.

Install:
1. Replace your current root index.html, style.css, script.js, manifest.webmanifest with these files.
2. Do not delete your existing assets/ folder.
3. Do not delete your existing sfx/ folder.
4. Deploy/test on Vercel or Spck preview.

Best test:
- Start EMX Core Grid.
- Confirm it says 1/15 waves.
- Check boss waves at 5, 10, and 15.
- Beat Wave 15 and confirm animated reward screen + Core Shards.
- Open Spend Shards and buy a permanent upgrade.
