EMX Tower Defense V8 Phase 5 SINGLE-PLAYER LIGHT

No sounds or asset images are included in this ZIP.
Keep your existing assets/ and sfx/ folders from the previous full project.

What changed:
- Shifted the main upgrade focus back to offline single-player depth.
- Added Phantom Glitches: invisible to standard towers unless detected by Prime Node, Shadow Sniper, Prism Scanner, or Scanner Hub.
- Added Armored Glitches: lead-style enemies that ignore standard/sharp hits unless you use explosive, heat, energy, prime, or corrosive damage.
- Added Prime Node hero tower: one per match, levels by XP after waves, unlocks Nova Scan at Level 3 and Annihilation Core at Level 10.
- Added Prime tab in the dock for Prime placement/info/abilities.
- Added dynamic map Overclock Tiles: towers placed on them get permanent attack-speed boost.
- Added decryptable Firewalls: tap a firewall and spend coins to open build space and line-of-sight.
- Added Firewalls as line-of-sight blockers until decrypted.
- Rebuilt Spend Shards into an EMX Motherboard skill tree style UI.
- Added new motherboard upgrades: Phantom Scanner BIOS, Armor-Pierce Driver, Prime Node Accelerator, Decrypt Toolkit, and Overclock Tile Tuning.
- Updated guide text for Phantom, Armored, Prime Node, Overclock Tiles, Firewalls, and Motherboard.

Install:
1. Replace your current root code files with these files.
2. Do NOT delete your existing assets/ folder.
3. Do NOT delete your existing sfx/ folder.
4. Run/deploy like normal through Spck/Vercel.

Files included here are code/config only.
